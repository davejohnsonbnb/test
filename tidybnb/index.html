Frequently asked questions

How much does a turnover clean cost?

Turnover cleaning starts from ₨3,000 and depends on the size of your property and the level of restocking needed. Send us your details for an exact, fixed quote.

Do you offer same-day turnovers?

Yes — same-day turnovers are available. We build flexible schedules around your bookings, so even tight same-day turnarounds are covered.

Which areas do you cover?

We serve short-term rentals in Lahore, Islamabad, Karachi and more areas across Pakistan. Not sure if we cover you? Just ask.

Do you restock supplies and report damage?

Yes. Restocking of toiletries and amenities is included, and we report any damage or low-stock items with photos.

Do I need to be there?

No. Most hosts manage remotely. We arrange secure access and keep you updated, so you never have to be on-site.

Can you handle multiple properties?

Absolutely — from a single flat to a portfolio. We scale to fit and keep every unit consistently guest-ready.
