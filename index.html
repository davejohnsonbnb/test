Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How much does a turnover clean cost?

Turnover cleaning typically ranges from ฿800–฿1,500 depending on the size of your property and the level of restocking needed. Send us your details for an exact, fixed quote.

Which areas do you cover?

We serve short-term rentals in Phuket, Koh Samui, the surrounding islands, and more areas across Thailand. Not sure if we cover you? Just ask.

Can you handle last-minute or same-day turnovers?

Wherever possible, yes. We build flexible schedules around your bookings — tell us your calendar and we'll do our best to fit tight turnarounds.

Do you restock supplies and report damage?

Yes. Restocking of toiletries and amenities is included, and we report any damage or low-stock items with photos so you're always in the loop.

Do I need to be there?

No. Most hosts manage remotely. We arrange secure access and keep you updated, so you never have to be on-site.
