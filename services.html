Our services

Everything your rental needs

From fast guest turnovers to deep resets, furniture care, odor removal and aircon treatment — all delivered by one reliable, professional team.

Turnover Cleaning

Our core service — resetting your rental between guests so it's flawless for every check-in. Consistent, fast, and reliable, following a photo-checked checklist.

  • Full clean of all rooms, kitchen and bathrooms
  • Fresh linens & beds made hotel-style
  • Towels, toiletries & amenities restocked
  • Rubbish removed & property left guest-ready
  • Damage & low-stock reporting with photos
✨

Deep Cleaning

A thorough, top-to-bottom reset for seasonal refreshes, long-stay checkouts, or getting a new listing photo-ready.

  • Inside appliances — oven, fridge, microwave
  • Grout, tiles, limescale & hard-water buildup
  • Behind & under furniture, skirting, vents
  • Windows, tracks, doors and high-touch detail
🛋️

Furniture Treatment

Keep sofas, upholstery and mattresses fresh, hygienic and looking new — protecting your furnishings and your guests' comfort.

  • Upholstery & sofa deep cleaning
  • Mattress cleaning & sanitising
  • Stain treatment & fabric refresh
  • Dust-mite & allergen reduction
🌬️

Odor Removal

Guest smoked indoors, or a lingering damp or cooking smell threatening your reviews? We neutralise odors at the source — not just mask them.

  • Cigarette & smoke odor elimination
  • Damp, musty & mildew smell treatment
  • Cooking & pet odor neutralising
  • Safe, guest-friendly treatments
❄️

HVAC & Aircon Treatment

In Thailand's climate, clean aircon is essential. We clean and sanitise units for cooler, healthier air and fewer guest complaints — and lower running costs.

  • Aircon filter & coil cleaning
  • Sanitising to remove mold, dust & smells
  • Improved cooling & energy efficiency
  • Routine maintenance schedules available
