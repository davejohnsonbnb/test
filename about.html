About us

Cleaning specialists for short-term rentals

Sabaify exists for one reason: to make short-term rental hosting effortless. We know a spotless property means better reviews, more bookings, and less stress — so that's exactly what we deliver.

Our story

Built around what hosts actually need

The name Sabaify comes from the Thai word sabai — comfortable, relaxed, at ease. That's the feeling we create: for your guests when they walk in, and for you knowing your property is in good hands.

We focus purely on short-term rentals, so we understand the pressure of tight turnovers, last-minute bookings and reviews that make or break a listing. Every clean follows a consistent, photo-checked process — nothing left to chance.

From a single condo to a portfolio of villas, we scale to fit, communicate clearly in English and Thai, and treat every property like it's our own.
